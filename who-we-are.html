Home / Who We Are

A button house in Kalkaji.

Ayush International has been importing and exporting buttons from Govindpuri since 2007. We do not tell a long company story — the work is the catalog, the lines, and the people who buy them.

Nature of business

Importer, exporter and wholesale supplier of buttons. Trade listings also note shell, seep, real M.O.P. and sea-shell ranges supplied from this address.

We sell to garment manufacturers, exporters, retail shops, and some individual customers. Orders are taken by line (size) and colour — mainly white, with rainbow and other shades where the type allows.

There is no separate published company history beyond the year the business started running on its own: 2013.
